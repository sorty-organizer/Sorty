# Unslop

Preserve the requested meaning, facts, tone, and format. Improve the text rather than adding commentary about editing it.

- Lead with the point. Prefer concrete nouns, active verbs, and familiar words.
- Cut puffery, filler, generic conclusions, sycophancy, and unsupported claims.
- Replace vague attributions with evidence or remove them. State uncertainty accurately.
- Keep terminology consistent. Split sentences that make the reader backtrack.
- Match the author's voice and the audience. Do not manufacture personality, opinions, or anecdotes.
- Use headings, lists, emphasis, and punctuation when they aid reading. Avoid repetitive templates, forced groups of three, and excessive formatting.
- Keep product copy brief and actionable. Preserve technical terms, quotations, and deliberate stylistic choices.

For a detailed editorial review or a stubborn wording pattern, consult the relevant section of [examples](references/examples.md). Ordinary edits need only this checklist.
